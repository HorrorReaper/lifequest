# Overall App
LifeQuest is a gamified journaling platform, which includes tasks, habits, daily plans and challenges. It helps you through making the process of becoming the best version of yourself more enjoyable, because you have clear measurements, which show you, that you improve.
It is developed to become the one personal operating system for your life. No need to switch between 10 different apps, just use LifeQuest

# Features
## Journaling
- Use predefined templates (like a morning reflection, daily review or evening review) or create your own template with 16 different kind of fields: Texts, Numbers, Slider, Star Ratings, Mood Picker, Single Choice, Yes/No Selection, Checklist, Task List, Day Planner and Habit Tracker
- Get notified at 8 AM in the morning for your morning briefing and at 8pm for your evening review
- You can mark text answers as ideas, wins, learnings and problems to filter them later. Also, you can mark numbers as metrics and see the development of these metrics over time
## Habits
- Create habits, track them every day and see the analytics around them. The more days in a row you complete them, the more xp and coins you will earn.
## Tasks
- Create tasks, update them and finish them. Get rewarded for every task you complete
## Daily Plan
- Plan your whole day the day before, in order to have everything you need the next day: What tasks you want to focus on and when.
## Articles
- get inspired through selected articles inside the app, which introduce you into new habits and methods/tools that will transform your life
## Challenges
- have guided challenges like the 14 day "Unfuck your life" Challenge, that will help you transform your life
## Goals/Quests 
- set Goals and big Quests, which motivate you over time
## Character
- Customize your character, give him different props
Appearance
- We have 3 different themes: the normal white mode, a nature inspired mode and a dark mode

# Why did I develope this app?
As a person that is obsessed with personal development, I never found the one application that really combined everything I need in order to become the best version of myself. I have used different apps like Me+ but it had not a gamification approach (as a past video game addict, I would love to gamify my life in order to keep track of my progress), but when I used Habitica, it had no daily planning. So that is why I created LifeQuest, which is designed to combine everything you need.
